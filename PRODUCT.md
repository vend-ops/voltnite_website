## Users and purpose

Prospective customers evaluating Voltnite's hardware, embedded systems, IoT, and cloud software engineering services. Visitors should understand the work, browse the three supplied projects, and prepare a project enquiry.

## Brand and design principles

Precise, practical, and dependable. The supplied homepage screenshot is the authoritative visual reference. Preserve its white and purple palette, typography, section sequence, compact proportions, and imagery. Do not redesign, introduce claims, or add testimonials or metrics.

## Accessibility

Semantic landmarks, keyboard navigation, visible focus, labelled forms, accessible error messages, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion support.
